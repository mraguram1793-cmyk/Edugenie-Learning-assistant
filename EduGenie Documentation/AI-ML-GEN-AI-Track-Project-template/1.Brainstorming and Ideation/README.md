## 🎯 Phase Objectives

- **Define the Problem** — Identify the core educational gap or challenge the project aims to solve
- **Empathize with Users** — Understand the target learner's perspective through empathy mapping
- **Generate & Prioritize Ideas** — Brainstorm solutions and filter down to the most impactful approach

---

## 🔍 Key Outcomes

- ✅ A well-defined problem statement grounded in real user needs
- ✅ An empathy map that guides user-centric design decisions
- ✅ A prioritized list of ideas that shaped the direction of EduGenie
